Learn a harmony
?How does this work?

This player gives you the melody and two harmonies. You can mute any part, or drop it an octave with the 8va button.

Learning a harmony for the first time:

  1. Mute the other parts and slow the tempo down.
  2. Learn your harmony on its own, as if it were a new melody.
  3. Speed back up, then unmute the other parts one at a time — keep singing your line while you listen to theirs.

If you get pulled off your line, that's normal — mute again, find it, and try once more.
